When a neuron’s groups are born

Each row is one group of a neuron’s 50 strongest contexts at the end of training; it is present at a training point when the median activation of its contexts is in the neuron’s top 0.1% there, and born at the first point where it is present and still present at the next.

Source: the verdict’s per-group record for the 600 OLMo-3 7B neurons of the development test, its births per training point, and the saved activations of each group’s contexts at the 16 training points.

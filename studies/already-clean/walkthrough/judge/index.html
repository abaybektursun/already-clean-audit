The judge’s calls

A judge saw each late-born group of a neuron’s contexts beside two records from earlier in training, its own neuron’s and a random neuron’s, and picked the one it grew from; each dot is one call, coloured by whether it picked the neuron’s own.

Source: the judge’s record of 7,352 calls on OLMo-3 7B against a random neuron, and the verdict’s per-group record, which holds the text the judge saw.

## Sentence rules (from the user's reference, zonderlabs.com/slop, read 10 November)

The reference names three faults of LLM writing. Check every sentence, caption, legend and hover line against them.

1. Hidden characters and actions. Who does what must be the subject and the verb. Not "a re-weighting of the control
   gives 0.206" but "we re-weighted the control and got 0.206". Not "the birth of a group is a line crossing" but "the
   neuron's activation on the cluster's tokens crosses a threshold". Turn nouns made from verbs back into verbs.
2. Vague wording: a pronoun or "that"/"it" whose referent the reader must hunt for; nouns stacked on nouns ("the
   input-replacement method's rule", "window-summed switch-off measurement"); words nobody uses for the thing ("the
   record", "the object", "reading" for a result). Use the ordinary word and say what the thing is.
3. Empty statements and misplaced emphasis. Lead with the point. Cut a sentence that states no fact. A reader who
   skims the first clause of each sentence should get the result.

## 7. Known pitfalls
- A new agent often ends its first turn with an empty summary and "No transcript found" on resume: respawn it
  with the same brief; tell it that its first action is a tool call.
- Agents' contexts get compacted after a few hours; they continue from their own summaries and STATE.md.
- The poller wakes on an AWS reconnect without a state change (cosmetic).
- `pg ps` lists only running instances; a launch missing from it has ended: read its STATUS on S3.
- EC2 RequestLimitExceeded when loops fire together: stagger by 60 s.
- Every bitwise bar across two computation paths has failed in this program (A30 on summation order; G_CAUSAL on
  batch shape, probably). Freeze a tolerance from a control pair, keep a hash check for the "differs by one
  step" failing input.

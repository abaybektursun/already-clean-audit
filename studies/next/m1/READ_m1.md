## 3. Discrepancies between the code and the spec

None of these changes the headline on these data.

1. **H_order when the lead is not SUPPORTED.** In `m1.verdict` (lines 484–492), R between G and M is labelled
   only in bands where the lead is SUPPORTED; other bands get `NOT_EVALUATED`. If H_lead is UNDECIDED or
   ABOVE_CEILING, H_order is set equal to H_lead. §1.3 says H_order is REFUTED if, in any band, the upper bound
   of Δ_RG or Δ_MR is below −bar, whatever the lead. §5 step 6 matches the code. No effect here: the lead is
   SUPPORTED in all four bands, so every band was evaluated.
2. **The headline re-reads only two failing inputs.** `no_room` (line 500) covers G_ID and PC_NLL only. §4
   says "The verdict reads every gate's failing input." The instance gates' failing inputs are asserted
   inside the job instead: G-SHARD at `m1_harvest.py` line 101, G-CFG at lines 118 and 123, G-CAST at line
   275. G-FIT's failing inputs exist offline only. No effect: I read every recorded failing input in the five
   `m1/metrics.json` and `controls.json` files, and each one failed (§4 below).
3. **G-SAME at the verdict compares against the harvest's own copy.** `fit_gate` is called with
   `rec["same"]` and `rec["nll_band"]` from each harvest's `m1/metrics.json` (line 458), not with `m1.SAME`
   and `m1.NLL_BAND`. §2.2 names `m1.SAME`. A job run with a planted registry would therefore pass the
   verdict's G-SAME. No effect: all five recorded copies equal `m1.SAME` and `NLL_BAND` at HEAD
   (`read/phase3.json` registry).
4. **One bootstrap index sequence for everything.** `boot_mean` (line 321) makes a fresh `default_rng(0)` for
   every call. Every band has 88,064 neurons, so every contrast and every band uses the same resample
   indices, and the intervals of different contrasts are not independent draws. This is allowed by the spec
   text. My independent RNG gives the same labels.
5. **Label order in `combine`.** The code checks ABOVE_CEILING before "all SUPPORTED"; §1.3 lists them the
   other way round. The two conditions cannot both hold, so the result is the same.
6. **Room check scope.** `m1.freeze` runs all 30 ordered pairs, including random init t00 and the identity
   pair t14b/t14c. §3.4 describes the offline check on neighbouring pairs. The freeze is stricter in coverage.
   Its pass needs only one SUPPORTED and one REFUTED anywhere.
7. **Spec facts that the data contradict.**
   - §3.6 says "each directory about 0.4M tokens of bands 1–2". `data/finemath-3plus` holds 791,610 tokens.
     One document (doc 12793) is 398,967 tokens long; the other 49 directories hold 400,006 to 437,393. So

8. **Banked samples come from one pole.** `slot_samples` takes the first slot of each kind in file order. So

## 7. Confounds and limits in the measurement

1. **The lead contrast is mostly G leaving code+math, not M arriving** (opening section). A mix-to-mix
   contrast cannot separate "M pulls toward code+math" from "G pushes away". The start is the only anchor,
   and against the start M barely moved. Every mix also changes the other groups (§8 threat 1). The web
   share falls by about as much as the code+math share rises: in G → M, the web total is −0.010 to −0.018
   by band and the PDF total −0.0025 to +0.0019 (`read/read_numbers.json` specific.dir_delta_MG).
2. **"Code+math" is mostly code.** By directory, the FineMath part of Δ_MG is −0.0011 and −0.0011 in bands
   0–1 and +0.0012 and +0.0021 in bands 2–3. C# and JavaScript carry +0.003 to +0.006 in every band, and
   Python carries +0.005 in bands 2–3 (`read/longdoc.json` dir_delta_MG_cm_dirs).
3. **Labels use the directory only (§8 threat 9).** In bands 2–3, two web directories moved toward M about
   as much as single code+math directories: `common_crawl-software_development-0019` (+0.0025, +0.0028) and
   `common_crawl-science_math_and_technology-0019` (+0.0012, +0.0031). That is consistent with a shift that
   follows code-like content, which directory labels undercount. These directories are also in the random
   sets of H_specific.
4. **One FineMath document is 1.9 % of the corpus.** Doc 12793 has 398,967 tokens of OCR text with long
   underscore runs. 57 of the 32,000 grid slots at M lie in it. In layers 20 and 26 it accounts for 0.0003
   and 0.0001 of Δ_MG = 0.0159 and 0.0113 (`read/longdoc.json`). It also holds 8 of the 32 code+math PC
   windows. Without those 8 windows, Δcm = −0.192 nats.
5. **The PC_NLL windows are not independent.** The 32 code+math windows start in 23 documents; up to 8
   windows share one document. A bootstrap over documents still fires: Δcm [−0.240, −0.082] and Δcm − Δweb
   [−0.334, −0.173] nats (`read/longdoc.json` pc_cluster_boot). M is better than G on 26 of 32 code+math
   windows and worse on 32 of 32 web windows (`read/phase3.json` per_window_*).
6. **UNDECIDED in H_order does not mean "the interval straddles the line".** In bands 0–1, Δ_MR's whole
   interval lies inside (−bar, +bar), and Δ_RG's interval in band 0 lies wholly below +bar. The frozen rule
   has no label for "R between G and M, but one gap is smaller than τ". More neurons or resamples cannot
   change this reading.
7. **The interval treats neurons as independent.** The tightest H_lead margin is band 0: the mean minus the
   bar is 0.0027, which is 5.0 interval half-widths (half-width 0.00054). Band 0 would become UNDECIDED only
   if dependence between neurons widened the interval more than 5-fold, which is a variance more than about
   25-fold. This was not measured. Eight layers per band are too few
   for a layer-level bootstrap.
8. **τ comes from stage 1.** The τ pairs are stage-1 checkpoints 1.0T to 1.85T tokens apart. The mixes are
   100B-token stage-2 runs. There is one end per mix and no seed replicate (§8 threat 7), so the run-to-run
   spread of a stage-2 end is unknown. A seed effect between the G and M runs cannot be told apart from the
   data effect.
9. **The identity floor is exactly zero.** The two start harvests are bit-identical, so bar = τ in every
   band. The manifests record the device (L40S) but no instance ID. Two separate runs are shown only by their
   timings and their distinct S3 attempts (mix-a-01, mix-b-01).
10. **A minority of poles drives the band mean.** At start_a, 28 % to 59 % of poles have cm_share = 0, by
    band. Under M − G, 30 % to 57 % of poles do not change (`read/read_numbers.json` per_pole).
11. **cm_share has a large baseline unrelated to training.** Random init (t00) has C = 0.25 to 0.31, against
    a code+math token share of 0.116. This does not confound M against G, but C is not a token-frequency
    quantity.
12. **Small items.** t15 has 2 poles with fewer than 50 filled slots (minimum 2). They enter the τ pair
    t14c–t15 (`read/phase3.json`). The corpus is stage-1-like web text (§8 threat 5).

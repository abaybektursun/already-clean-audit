## 0. What the program knows, in the words this spec uses

The REUSE program measured, on `allenai/Olmo-3-1025-7B` (32 layers, MLP width 11,008, 16 checkpoints T_0 to T_15), how a neuron's top-activating passages split into clusters (the W4 clusterer's "jobs"), when each cluster is born (UNITE's rule: the first checkpoint at which the cluster's median activation reaches the neuron's corpus 99.9th percentile), and whether one cluster depends on another's upstream drivers (direct attribution at the marked token, top 50 upstream MLP neurons, mean-replaced in a 256-token window; the fall on the other cluster over the fall on its own drivers).

Three facts of that program are the premises here. Each is a number with an artifact.

1. **The controls' generic term comes from a few dozen within-layer outlier neurons, and it does not run through the norm.** The top 1 percent of upstream neurons by background activation |ā| (3,412 of 341,248; `results/birth/verdict_birth.json`) carry 60 percent of the two-neuron null's rise over training; with them excluded the null at T_15 falls from 0.163 [0.110, 0.220] to 0.065 [0.034, 0.088], its 95th percentile from 0.468 to 0.376 (Results 24 to 27). The members that enter attribution-ranked sets are 10 to 131 distinct neurons per load, mostly in layers 8 to 25, at 129 to 639 times their own layer's median |ā| (Addendum 1). Holding the post-feedforward norm's divisor during the replacement does not lower the controls (SPEC6, Results 38 to 43: the unexcluded null 0.130 to 0.188 held; the gap between the unexcluded and excluded nulls 0.092 free, 0.074 held), so the norm-scale account is withdrawn and "direct" is not established. The replacement code supports two written values, `mean` and `zero` (`infra/pg/reuse_ablate.py:57`, `ablation_dict`); every result of the program used `mean`.

2. **A neuron's clusters share about an eighth of their drivers, and the dependence follows the shared drivers, not the old–new order.** Overlap 0.14 (newer with older) and 0.125 (with the neuron's other clusters) at T_15 against a two-neuron 95th percentile of 0.06 and a split-cluster 0.38. The ratio I = Δ(D′(K); J)/Δ(D′(J); J) is 0.24 to 0.33 at T_15 and 0.21 to 0.25 at the birth for any other cluster K of the neuron, the same as for the older cluster (SPEC10, SPEC11; H_old refuted), and fits I ≈ 0.03 + 1.70 s at the birth and 0.08 + 1.45 s at T_15, s the shared fraction of the two top-50 sets. The weights alone do not name the drivers (SPEC6 rule W1: weights-only top 50 overlaps D′(J) by 0.08 against a null 95th percentile of 0.06); the weights with one activity statistic folded in (mean |a_k| · s_l · ω_k, `results/sanity/s4_sameweights.py`) reproduce D′(J) at 0.64.

3. **The object is the clusterer's, and a reader does not see most of its jobs as one role.** A Sonnet 5.5 reader passes constructed controls (same-token sets 27 of 30 coherent, mixed sets 30 of 30 mixed) and finds 20 of 129 O* jobs coherent in both halves, 43 of 150 J sets coherent (Result 19). No driver-based definition of a job has been tried. The per-context attribution A [N, L·W] is computed in `reuse_ablate.drivers_direct` (line 190) and discarded; only each side's summed top 200 is banked.

On `Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B` (ALWAYSON): the released loop edit (Takehi, Teoh, Zhang and Khattab, `studies/alwayson/sources/`) subtracts a direction u from 64 `down_proj` columns in each of blocks 15 and 19, W ← W − 6 u(uᵀW), the columns chosen by score(n) = (u · W[:, n]) × (mean activation of n on repetitive contexts − on productive contexts). The columns are not high-background (SPEC1), the 64 largest-|ā| columns carry a random-level share of Σ(u·W)² (1.3 and 2.0 percent), and under the exploratory repetition reading the editor's edit lowers the repetition share by 0.305 [0.220, 0.385] against a norm-matched random set's 0.050 (SPEC2, Results 7 to 12). The frozen loop flag saturated at the 4,096-token cap (198 of 200 base answers cut inside the thinking block), a spec defect. The matched Frobenius norm was not a matched realized change: the always-on arm's samples departed from the base arm's earliest of the three column sets (departure tokens 69.5, 103, 135). The generations of all five arms are banked (`studies/alwayson/results/edit/1/generations/A0.jsonl` to `A4.jsonl`, with per-answer token ids and flags), as are u per block and ā per neuron (`studies/alwayson/results/1/abar.npz`: `u` [2, 1024], `u_proj` [2, 3584], `abar`, `mabs` [24, 3584]).

Banks that make this spec cheap: the grid bank of every Olmo-3 neuron at every checkpoint (top-50 contexts, log10|a| histograms with q99/q999/q9999, sign counts; `../interp_research-neurons-harvest/bank/neurons/w3/grid/{1,1b}/out/t<T>_step<S>/{topk,stats,poles}`); harvest banks on the same corpus for `google/gemma-3-4b-pt` (300 sampled neurons) and `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Base` (360 sampled neurons) with `topk`, `stats`, `poles` (`.../bank/neurons/w1/{g4,q4b}/1/out/`); the clusterer's partitions for about 300 distinct Olmo poles (primary and fresh in `../interp_research-neurons-explain/bank/w4/o7_s_v2/explain`, low-layer in `studies/reuse/results/lowlayer/clusters/explain/clusters.jsonl` with two samples per pole, third set and dating set in their study banks); SPEC4's 56 planted two-neuron pairs and 150 primary cases with their arms at T_15 (`results/birth/verdict_birth.json`: `pairs_T15`, `cases`).

## 1. The abstract frame, the questions, the decisions

Every instrument of mechanistic interpretability does one of three things: it **writes a value** into the model (an ablation), it **trusts a ranking** (an attribution), or it **assumes a partition** (a feature, a cluster, a job). The program's results rest on one of each: the mean written into upstream neurons, the direct-attribution top 50, the clusterer's jobs. None of the three was tested against something outside itself. This spec tests each against the strongest available reference:

| instrument | what it assumes | the reference it is read against | experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| mean replacement | the mean is a neutral value to write | the same sets under zero, median and resample values; a first-order prediction of each; two other models | E1 |
| direct attribution as a ranking | activation × alignment names the units that carry a behaviour | a released, externally evaluated weight edit (a ground truth set) and the two single-factor rankings | E2 |
| the clusterer's partition | the text split is the neuron's division of labour | a partition from the neuron's own inputs (driver vectors), a token-identity partition, a permutation null, the clusterer's self-agreement, a reader | E3 (reader stage E0) |
| the shared-driver fit | collateral is predictable from driver overlap | held-out cases on driver-defined clusters under the winning replacement rule | E4, conditional on E3 |

**The three questions, each with its decision.**

- **Q1 (E1).** Is the generic term of mean-replacement controls a property of the written value? Decision: which replacement rule every ablation in this program and the method note recommend; whether the headline reuse ratio is value-dependent; whether the term is a first-order effect (direct) or not. The practitioner's number is the false-positive line (the two-neuron null's 95th percentile) under each rule on three models.
- **Q2 (E2).** Which factor of a first-order attribution carries the realized effect of a projective weight edit, and what does the edit cost per unit effect? Decision: whether the program's attribution instrument is a recipe for finding edit targets without the editor's contexts; whether activations are needed at all (the weights-only reading); how controls for edits must be matched (norm or realized change).
- **Q3 (E3).** Is a neuron's division into tasks visible in its inputs or only in the text? Decision: whether the program's object (a job) is restated on a label-free, mechanistic definition; whether the eighth-shared result is about the neuron or about the clusterer; whether a detector of multi-job neurons exists without an LLM.

Each question's null and positive outcomes lead to different next actions (research-spec Phase 0, item 2); none is run for corroboration alone.

**Why these three and not the birth line.** The birth line is closed on three threshold-free readings (SPEC8, SPEC12); a planted-drift positive control could reopen a question with thin application. A larger fresh set, another LLM relatedness reader, and attention heads before a smaller perturbation is specified spend without deciding anything. The three experiments here each change what the program says and each yields a number a practitioner uses.

## 3. E1: the written value in a mean-replacement control

### 3.1 Hypotheses and refuting observations

Let N(v, e) be the two-neuron null's level (median over 56 pairs of the cross fall over the own fall, both sides read with the value v written) under set construction e ∈ {unexcluded, excluded}, and G(v) = N(v, unexcluded) − N(v, excluded), paired over pairs. Under the mean, G(mean) = 0.098 (0.163 − 0.065; like for like on SPEC6's pairs 0.092).

| hypothesis | says | refuting observation |
|---|---|---|
| H_value | the term is a first-order push of an atypical written value into heavy-tailed units; a value drawn from the unit's own distribution removes it | G(resample) is not below half of G(mean): the paired difference G(mean) − G(resample) has its interval below 0.049 |
| H_set | the term belongs to the members (any perturbation of a load-bearing unit moves the pole); the value does not matter | G(resample) ≤ 0.5 G(mean), or the members-alone fall under resample is under half of its mean-rule value |
| H_zero (a corollary of H_value) | for sparse heavy-tailed units zero is a typical value, so zero replacement removes the term as resample does | G(zero) above half of G(mean) with its interval above 0.049 |
| H_linear | the members-alone fall is predicted by Σ_k (v_k − a_k(x)) c_k(x), c_k the direct attribution coefficient at the marked token | Pearson r under 0.3 across sets × values, or a slope interval excluding (0.5, 2) |
| H_routes | exclusion and resampling are two routes to the same reuse ratio on the real cases | I_unexcluded(resample) − I_excluded(mean) on the 150 primary cases has its interval outside [−0.05, 0.05] |
| H_model | the term and the value-dependence are not Olmo-specific | on gemma-3-4b-pt or Qwen3-4B-Base, G(mean) under 0.03 (no term to remove) or G(mean) − G(resample) with its interval below half of that model's G(mean) |

H_value and H_set predict opposite outcomes on G(resample). The boring hypothesis is H_set.

### 3.2 The measurement

**Items.** SPEC4's 56 planted two-neuron pairs at T_15, re-read from `results/birth/verdict_birth.json` `pairs_T15` (same poles, same contexts, same seeds); SPEC4's 150 primary cases at T_15 (`cases`, the J and O* sides, 12 contexts each). Nothing new is drawn on Olmo.

**Arms per side.** identity (empty set), own (D′ of the side), cross (D′ of the other side), random (the SPEC4 random-matched construction), members-alone (the cross set's high-background members alone, `reuse_generic.members_alone`), each under construction e ∈ {unexcluded, 1%-excluded (SPEC4's global B_t mask)} where the construction applies, and each under four written values v:

- `mean`: ā_k, the mean over the 200 background positions (REUSED, `reuse_ablate.background_mean_a`);
- `zero`: 0 (REUSED);
- `median`: the median over the same 200 background positions (NEW);
- `resample`: for each replaced neuron one value drawn uniformly from its 200 banked background activations, one draw per forward, written at every position ≥ start as the mean is; R = 4 draws (seeds 0 to 3) and the fall averaged over draws (NEW).

The values come from one capture of the 200 background positions kept as a [200, L, W] fp32 tensor (282 MB at Olmo's size); the builder asserts the tensor's mean over positions equals the banked ā to 1e-5 (the known number of the capture).

**Readouts.** For each pair and side, per (v, e): the fall Δ = a_P(before) − a_P(after) on the side's contexts (REUSED `reuse_ablate.readout`, `fall`, `job_fall`), the cross/own ratio, the members-alone fall. For each primary case per (v, e): own, cross, random, the ratio I. For the first-order prediction: at the marked token of every context, a_k(x) and c_k(x) for every neuron of every set in play, c the coefficient vector such that A = a ⊙ c (NEW: the builder exposes c from the same code path that computes A in `unite_route.attribution`, asserted by A == a ⊙ c to 1e-5 relative); predicted Δ_lin = Σ_{k ∈ S} (v_k − a_k(x)) c_k(x), summed then averaged over the side's contexts, sign convention the same as the measured fall.

**Summaries.** N(v, e) as the median over pairs; G(v) paired over pairs; bootstrap over pairs (2,000 draws, seed 0) for every interval. I on primary cases as the median over cases, bootstrap over neurons. r and slope by least squares over (set × value) points of the members-alone arm and the own arm, with a bootstrap over pairs.

## 4. E2: which factor of an attribution carries a weight edit

### 4.1 Hypotheses and refuting observations

For each arm a, R_a = rep_base − rep_a, the paired reduction of the repetition share (§4.2). E is the editor's released 64 per block; D, A, P are the direction-only, activation-only and product rankings computed on the base model's own generations (§4.2); P_k the product ranking at k ∈ {16, 64, 256}; R_norm and R_KL the random 64 at the norm-matched and the KL-matched strength.

| hypothesis | says | refuting observation |
|---|---|---|
| H_recipe | the product ranking on the model's own loops reproduces the released edit without the editor's contexts | R_P64 / R_E with its interval below 0.8 |
| H_product | both factors are needed: the product beats either single factor | R_P64 − max(R_D, R_A) with its interval below 0 |
| H_direction | the weights alone name the targets (the weight-space reading) | R_E − R_D with its interval above 0.10 |
| H_dose | the effect and the collateral both rise with k, and the effect per unit loss falls | R_P256 ≤ R_P64, or Δloss_P256 ≤ Δloss_P64 |
| H_match | a norm-matched random control understates a matched-realized-change control | R_KL − R_norm with its interval below 0 |
| H_boring | the editor's 64 are special beyond their score (selection on their contexts, or on accuracy) | H_recipe refuted while the recovery count, the size of P64 ∩ E, is above 64 of 128 (the same columns, a different effect: then the contexts are not the difference, and the difference is cancelled as unexplained) |

### 4.2 The measurement

**Model and decoding.** `Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B` at revision `2fc06364715b967f1860aea9cf38778875588b17`, bfloat16, transformers 5.20.2 installed in-job, `attn_implementation="sdpa"`, chat template with thinking on, the editor's prompt suffix, sampling temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95, top_k 20 (REUSED `jobs/alwayson_edit.py`), with two changes frozen here: **cap 8,192 new tokens** and **a presence penalty of 1.5** applied by a logits processor (NEW: at each step, subtract 1.5 from the logit of every token id that has appeared in the generated ids so far; unit test: after generating id t, the next step's logit at t is lower by 1.5 than the unprocessed logit; the editor's vLLM eval used 1.5). One seed per batch as SPEC2 (1000 + the batch's first problem index); batches of 50; prompts left-padded.

**Problems.** MATH-500 test, the same seed-0 permutation as SPEC2; the first **300** rows (SPEC2's 200 plus the next 100); the pilot is the first batch of 50 on two arms (base, E).

**Per answer** (REUSED `alwayson_edit.answer_flags`, extended): token ids, text, new-token count, cap hit, boxed after thinking, rep flag (a 10-gram three times at equal intervals within 100 tokens), the **onset** (NEW: the position of the first token of the first firing 10-gram's first occurrence; cap if none), accuracy (NEW: `normalise(boxed_last(after_think))` equals `normalise(answer)`).

**Arms (9).** base; E (editor's 64 per block at strength 6); D (top 64 per block by |u · W[:, n]|, SPEC1's C_dir); A (top 64 by Δa_n = mean a_n on looping positions − on productive positions, descending); P16, P64, P256 (top k by (u · W[:, n]) × Δa_n, descending); R_norm (64 random per block, seed 0, at the strength matching P64's ‖ΔW‖_F per block, REUSED `strength_matching`); R_KL (the same 64 at the strength s_R such that KL(base ‖ edited) on 200 held-out corpus windows equals P64's within 5 percent, found by bisection over s on a log grid, at most 12 forwards per block pair). Every arm except base projects u out of its columns with the editor's formula W ← W − s u(uᵀW) (REUSED `edit_columns`); u per block from `abar.npz` `u`.

**The contexts for A and P.** The base arm's 200 generations of SPEC2 (`generations/A0.jsonl`, token ids), re-run teacher-forced through the base model with hooks on blocks 15 and 19 `down_proj` inputs; a position is **looping** if it lies at or after the onset of its answer's first firing 10-gram, **productive** otherwise (every position of an answer with no firing is productive); prompt positions excluded. Δa_n per block from these two means. The editor's contexts are not available and not used; that is the point.

**Collateral per arm** (one forward each, no generation): mean NLL per token on 500 held-out windows of 512 tokens drawn with seed 1 from the W1 corpus (`s3://<s3-bucket>/results/neurons/w1/q4b/1/out/corpus/corpus/docs.jsonl.gz`, documents disjoint from SPEC1's 2,000 windows), Δloss = NLL_arm − NLL_base with a paired bootstrap over windows; mean KL(base ‖ arm) per token on the same windows.

**Summaries.** rep_a and R_a with a paired bootstrap over problems (2,000 draws, seed 0); the onset's Kaplan–Meier median per arm with the cap as censoring; accuracy per arm; Δloss and KL per arm; effect per collateral R_a / Δloss_a.

### 4.4 Frozen rules (lines set on SPEC2's data; three-way readings)

- **R1 (positive control).** R_E's interval above 0.15 → the rules below are read; otherwise every rule is **cancelled** and the pivot taken.
- **R2 (H_recipe).** Q = R_P64 / R_E (ratio of paired means; the bootstrap draws the problems once for both). Interval above 0.8 → holds; entirely below 0.8 → refuted; otherwise not separated.
- **R3 (H_product).** D = R_P64 − max(R_D, R_A), paired. Interval above 0.05 → holds; entirely below 0 → refuted; otherwise not separated.
- **R4 (H_direction).** D = R_E − R_D, paired. Interval below 0.05 → the direction suffices; entirely above 0.10 → refuted; otherwise not separated.
- **R5 (H_dose, reported).** R_P16, R_P64, R_P256 and Δloss at each; H_dose refuted on either monotonicity failure (point estimates).
- **R6 (H_match).** D = R_KL − R_norm, paired. Interval above 0 → holds; entirely below 0 → refuted; otherwise not separated.
- **Power rule.** From the pilot's paired standard deviation of rep flags on base and E, project the half-width of R's interval at n = 300; if above 0.08, the full run takes all 500 problems (a dated amendment before the full run; the lines do not change).

## 5. E3: a job defined by the neuron's inputs (with the reader stage E0)

### 5.1 Hypotheses and refuting observations

For a pole P with 50 banked top contexts at T_15 and the clusterer's partition C (sample 0), let V be the partition of the same contexts by their driver vectors (§5.2), K the partition by marked-token string, and ARI the adjusted Rand index.

| hypothesis | says | refuting observation |
|---|---|---|
| H_input | the clusterer's split is visible in the inputs: V agrees with C beyond the token partition | ARI(V, C) − ARI(K, C), median over poles, interval below 0 |
| H_token | the input structure seen is token identity | ARI(V, C) − ARI(K, C) interval above 0.05 |
| H_predict | the driver partition predicts the clusterer's label of a held-out context better than the token does | held-out accuracy (driver) − (token), median over poles, interval below 0 |
| H_read | driver-defined sets read as one role no more often than the clusterer's | paired coherent-share difference (driver sets − clusterer sets) with its interval above 0.10 or below −0.10 |
| H_depth (E0) | low-layer (0 to 3) clusterer jobs read as coherent more often than deeper jobs | coherent share, low-layer minus deeper, interval below 0.15 |
| H_boring | V is noise: its agreement with C sits at the permutation null | the share of poles with ARI(V, C) above their own null's 95th percentile under 0.15 (three times the null's 0.05) |

### 5.2 The measurement

**Items.** Every Olmo pole with a banked clusterer partition and 50 banked T_15 contexts: the primary and fresh poles (`../interp_research-neurons-explain/bank/w4/o7_s_v2/explain`), the third set (`studies/reuse/inputs/birthobject_items.json`, 80 poles), the low-layer set (`studies/reuse/results/lowlayer/clusters/explain/clusters.jsonl`, 128 poles, two samples each), the dating set (`inputs/dating_items.json`, 183 poles); about 300 distinct poles after the union (the items builder counts them and banks the list with a hash). ## 11. Coordination protocol (how Fable 5.1 runs this with Opus 5.5 subagents)

**The division.** The coordinator decides, reads gates and samples, recounts, and writes the result documents. Subagents do all building, launching, watching, scoring and reviewing. The coordinator never builds code beyond a recount script of under 150 lines and never launches an instance; a subagent never decides a rule's reading or a pivot.

**The coordinator's context, kept small on purpose.** At any time the coordinator holds: this spec; `studies/instruments/STATE.md` (one page, rewritten by the coordinator at every stage boundary: the run/unrun/parked count over the roster, open jobs with instance ids, the next action, the open questions); the last stage's `metrics.json` gate block; the samples file; the verdict JSON's rule block; the builder's `BUILD_NOTES_<stage>.md` (at most 60 lines, fixed sections: what was built, the dry run's printed lines, deviations from the spec, open questions); the read's `READ_<stage>.md`. **Subagents.** Opus 5.5 only (`model: "opus"`), at most two at once, each in its own worktree (`../interp_research-inst-<stage>`, branch `research/inst-<stage>`), one writer per file. Roles, one per agent:

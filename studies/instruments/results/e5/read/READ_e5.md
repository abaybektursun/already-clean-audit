## Gates
4. **C, with a caveat. held_identity.** On the clean forward the hold multiplies by exactly 1, so this check passes by construction. It would also pass if the hold did nothing on the ablated forward. Three things show the hold acted. (a) The shifted scales fail on every pair (min 0.268, max 1.63, line 1e-4). (b) None of the 336 held, held1 and marked arms is bitwise equal to its free arm; the smallest change is 0.008 of max a_P. (c) `done()` asserts that every reader replayed its batch. **The real run banked no probe of the input each reader received.** The samples' `held_rms_clean_ablated` holds the clean RMS and the ablated RMS before the hold: the correction applied, not the result. Its ablated/clean ratio at the target layer is 0.90 to 0.96 for own and 0.98 to 1.01 for cross, on 3 layer-5 pairs only. The received-RMS probe ran only on the tiny dry-run model (2.2e-3) and in the selfcheck. On Olmo the clean RMS at the reader is guaranteed by the code (fp32 multiply, then a bf16 cast), not observed.

## Attacks
8. **D. T1 "a third" is partly a global attenuation.** The hold also lowers the own fall: per pair, held own / free own is 0.870 [0.851, 0.906]. The cross/own ratio falls from 0.156 to 0.108 (ratio of medians), from 0.163 to 0.112 (tercile null) and from 0.130 to 0.101 (per-pair median): a drop of 22 to 31 percent. Expected cross fall if the hold scaled it by the own set's factor, minus the held cross fall: 0.0140 [0.0054, 0.0216], which is 19 percent of 0.0741. So 0.0247 / 0.0741 = 0.33 is right in absolute terms. The part specific to the cross term is a fifth to three tenths. By depth: at layers 15 and 26, where the term is, the hold removes about half (0.127 to 0.069; 0.100 to 0.045). At layer 5 it removes almost nothing (0.0185 to 0.0175).
9. **D. T1's part sits at the target's layer, not upstream.** Holding only the target layer removes 0.0314 [0.0185, 0.0450], at least as much as the all-layer hold (0.0247). Held minus held1 cross is +0.0014 [0.0003, 0.0038] (seed 1 [0.0003, 0.0039]; 66 percent of pairs): adding the upstream layers to the hold leaves slightly more fall. So the upstream readers' raw inputs carry none of the term. The removed part is at layer L: v at every position and gate/up at the marked token (q and k are no-ops, amendment 43). Supported wording: "the target layer reads a marked-token input whose RMS the cross write moved." E5 does not show that missing norms on the path between the ablated layer and the target transmit the term.
10. **C, with gaps. T2 is comparable.** The marked write uses the same `IV.written` values as the free write, and the post-hook asserts that down_proj got them at (row, marked position, idx) with every other entry unchanged. BOS (position 0) is unwritten and unheld in every arm. Not banked: a write at the marked token plus k neighbours, or at every position except the first content token. So T2 cannot say which of the other positions carry the term. A finding the text omits: free minus marked is 0.0599 [0.0393, 0.0872] on the own set against 0.0675 on the cross set. The other-position push takes about 0.06 of a_P out of either knock-out; it is also inside the own fall. For scale, E1's layer-matched random sets fall 0.027.
11. **C. T4 is comparable on what matters, not on depth.** λ median 0.96 against E1's 0.92. B_t members per J cross set 2.48 against 2.41 (pairs with a member: 36 against 33). m 50 and 12 contexts on both. Layers: T4 spans 29 layers from 1 to 31, E1 uses 5, 15 and 26. Per-tercile ratio: T4 0.129 / 0.110 / 0.214, E1 0.039 / 0.277 / 0.215. **0.128 against 0.163 is sampling.** The difference is 0.035 [-0.100, 0.117], and under the plain median the order flips (0.158 against 0.130). Cross-fall medians are 0.0739 and 0.0741. "The construction is not the difference" stands.
12. **D. T5's units.** The statistic divides an L2 norm (|ā| ||d|| s) by a per-coordinate RMS (sqrt(mean h²)). In L2-over-L2 units it is the statistic over √d: Olmo 0.0140, gemma 0.0017, Qwen 0.0022. An Olmo member writes 1.4 percent of ||h||, not something near the residual's size, as "0.90 of the residual's RMS" reads. √d also differs between the models (64 against 50.6), which inflates Olmo's ratio by 1.27: 10.3x becomes 8.1x.
13. **D. T5's "ten times" depends on the normalization.** Olmo over gemma, by statistic: per distinct member, median 10.3x, p90 3.0x, maximum 0.54x (gemma's largest member 8.8 against Olmo's 4.7); matched on count (gemma's top 34 against Olmo's 34) 1.12x; members' write summed per set 2.0x (7.66 against 3.83, over 33 and 55 pairs); per set-slot 5.9x; relative to the RMS at the target's layer, the reader's, 6.2x; the change |a − ā| in place of |ā|, from the banked 4 draws, 6.3x; member |ā| over the median |ā| of the same set's ordinary neurons 0.40x (116x against 285x; this ignores column norms, which are not banked for ordinary neurons). The order of magnitude holds only at the per-member median. That median depends on gemma's 189 members including many small ones; matched on count, the gap is gone. Residual-scale growth over the upstream layers in this bank: 22x (Olmo) against 88x (gemma). The per-layer normalization removes it at the member's own layer, but the members sit at different depths (median layer 21.5 of 32, 15 of 34 and 29 of 36).
14. **D. "Cause 5 ... is supported" and "the leading candidate for the rest."** T5's registered reading has two outcomes, argued against or not argued against; "supported" is not one of them. Given item 13, and given that gemma's units of the same kind are inert (members-alone fall 0.0016 against 0.088, OLMO_DIFFERENCES B1), T5 cannot apportion "the rest". Panel c of the figure keeps the registered wording.

## Suggestions
26. **S. The Decision does not answer its second half: should Olmo's instrument change?** Under the marked-token write, cross/own is 0.0137 [0.0049, 0.0266] against 0.156 under the free write. On the banked numbers the marked-token write is the better Olmo control; it also removes 14 percent of the own fall (item 10).
27. **S. The sign of the push.** In the 3 banked pairs, the own write lowers the target-layer RMS by 4 to 6 percent and the cross write by 0 to 1 percent. Mean replacement removes per-token variance, which lowers the RMS on average. For a target without an input norm, that predicts a one-signed fall for the T1 part. Test: bank the ablated/clean RMS at the target layer for all 56 pairs; the hold's hooks already compute it, so no new arm is needed.
28. **S. Two arms that would close items 9 and 10.** The layer-L hold split by reader (v against gate/up), and a write at the marked token plus k neighbours.

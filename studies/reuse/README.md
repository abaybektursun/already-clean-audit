# REUSE — does a neuron's new job run through the old job's upstream inputs?

**Answer after SPEC11 (2026-11-03), in measured terms: a neuron's clusters share about an eighth of their top-50 drivers, and replacing one cluster's drivers lowers the neuron on another by about a quarter of its own-driver fall, rising with the drivers they share (the newer cluster on the older cluster's drivers 0.24 to 0.30 at the end of training and 0.17 to 0.26 at its birth, 0.17 and 0.22 on the 83 cases with another existing cluster; on the pole's other clusters' drivers 0.24 to 0.33 at the end and 0.21 to 0.25 at the birth; two unrelated neurons 0.01 at birth and 0.07 at the end). The newer cluster's dependence on the older cluster's drivers is not larger, by the frozen line, than on the other clusters UNITE's pairings link to it, at the end of training (SPEC10) or at its birth (SPEC11, where 62 of the 101 clusters read were born at the same checkpoint; on the 39 older ones the difference is −0.030 [−0.073, 0.017], exploratory), so the old–new pairing of the first headline is dropped; whether it is a property of the neuron beyond that lineage was not tested.**

## 1. Question, decision, hypotheses

Question: when a neuron in layers 0 to 3 of Olmo-3-7B takes on a second cluster of contexts (UNITE's birth), does the new cluster's rise against the older cluster lock to the birth checkpoint, as an event, where deeper neurons' clusters drift? Decision: if the low-layer birth is an event, SPEC8's drift conclusion is scoped to layers 4 and up and the acquisition of token-level jobs becomes a successor program's object (a discrete acquisition at a datable step, with the per-context locking as its signature); if not, the drift conclusion covers all layers and the exploratory low-layer lead was the late-birth artifact its within-stratum null suggested.

| hypothesis | prediction on the new low-layer set | refuted by |
|---|---|---|
| H_token-event: the low-layer birth is a locked event | E1's interval above the within-set shuffled null's 95th percentile and the gain into the birth over elsewhere with its interval above 1 | E1's interval at or below the null's 95th percentile, or the gain's interval containing or below 1 |
| H_context-event: the low-layer contexts of J jump together more than the same pole's mixed contexts (a context-level signature without a locked ratio break) | E2's seed-free difference with its interval above 0 while E1 or the gain fails | E2's interval containing or below 0 |
| H_same: low-layer births read as deeper births do (drift) | E1 not above its null, the gain containing 1, E2 at 0 | any of E1, the gain or E2 firing |
| H_artifact: the exploratory lead came from the late births and the nested document factor | with separate documents and a within-set null, E1 and the gain read as H_same | E1 and the gain firing on the new set |

E1 decides between H_token-event and the rest; the gain confirms it; E2 separates H_context-event from H_same. The deeper comparison is SPEC5's 240 cases (layers 4 to 31, separate documents, the same instrument: E1 0.275 [0.197, 0.353] and 0.341 [0.236, 0.444] against shuffled 95th percentiles 0.268 and 0.330; gain over elsewhere 0.97 on the fresh set), reported beside the new set, no rule.

Clustering: the W4 clusterer unchanged (prompt v2, `neurons_prompts/clusterer.md`, sha256 `5d8b55ca…`; model `claude-sonnet-5-5`; two samples per pole, the same seed rule; each answer a partition of ranks 0 to 49; the double-negative echo rule), the requests prepared by `neurons_explain.py prepare-clusters` and answered through the manual backend by Sonnet subagents (the W4 route of 2026-10-21, $0 API), collected by `collect-clusters` with its validity checks and re-asks; the clusterer's answers banked as `clusters.jsonl` with `calls.jsonl`. **Readout (SPEC8 §2 unchanged, on the new cases).** J/O*(t), the break of log J/O* (Chen et al. 2024 eq. 2, Δ = 1) on valid t, the jump time of each context under c_x(t) = a_P(x, t) / g_l(t), the log-rate per log-step; validity and liveness as SPEC8 (s_P(t) > 0.01 s_P(T_15); mean a_P over O* > 0 at t_b; the pairs' validity). ## 3. Design

Discovery and confirmation: the rules' forms are SPEC8's, frozen there; the lines are the within-set null (a procedure, not a level), 1 for the gain, 0 for E2; nothing is tuned on the new set. The capture is launched before the clustering is finished (the capture takes all 50 positions per pole, so it does not depend on the clusters), and the items are built only after the verdict script is frozen (`reuse-freeze-12b`). Sample size: at the existing harvest's yield of 1.38 separate-document qualifying cases per low-layer neuron (51 cases from 37 neurons; 11 neurons give none), 64 neurons give about 88 cases on about 45 neurons (5th percentile about 63). Floor: fewer than 40 live cases or fewer than 20 neurons → every rule "not read" (underpowered), the harvest kept for a successor. Cost: 16 loads with the forward stopped after layer 3, about 6,800 contexts per load in two windows; about 2.5 GPU-h on a g6e.2xlarge, downloads dominating; 256 clusterer requests at $0 API; one builder.

## 5. Gates (each with its failing input and an identity value where one exists; a failed gate stamps every rule "not read")

- Known number (harvest condition), per load: the known poles' a_h against their banked `exemplar_a` at that load, median relative difference over the 400 contexts at most 0.02 (bf16 noise; at T_15 the new poles' a_h against the banked `topk.a`, the same line); failing input: the previous load's bank.
- Known number (SPEC8 instrument), per load where a known pole has a third-set case: a_P on that case's J and O* contexts against SPEC8's banked `dating/<pole>__c<c>.npz`, median relative difference at most 0.05; failing input: the next load's bank.
- Capture identity, per load: the first pole's first 20 positions captured twice, max abs difference 0 in a_h and under 1e-5 in a_P; failing input: the hook on layer L + 1 (layer 0 has no layer below), which must differ by more than 1e-5.
- Completeness, per load: 136 poles × 50 positions present, every a_h and a_P finite.
- Clusterer validity: every collected answer partitions ranks 0 to 49, the echo rule holds, both samples present for every pole; re-asks and refusals counted; failing input: an answer with a duplicated rank, which `collect-clusters` must reject.
- Dating known number: the re-dating of the 74 existing low-layer poles' clusters reproduces `q2_clusters.jsonl`'s births and late_primary flags with 0 mismatches; failing input: q999 read from the next checkpoint, which must change at least one birth.
- Global scale: g_l(t) > 0 at every load for l in 0 to 3, read from SPEC8's bank with its sha256; failing input: a load's file replaced by another load's, which the sha256 check must refuse.
- Scale validity: s_P(T_15) > 0 for every case; the share of valid (case, t) pairs from T_9 on reported, with the line 0.90 from SPEC8 (set on its set; reported, and under it the rules are "not read").
- Null power: the within-set shuffled-dates null of E1 has a 95th percentile below 0.5 and above 0.1 (room on both sides); failing input: the observed dates shuffled, which must land inside the null.
- Case count: at least 40 live cases on at least 20 neurons.

## 6. Frozen rules (forms from SPEC8 §6; bootstraps over neurons, 2,000 draws, seed 0, 95 percent percentile intervals)

- **E1 (decides).** Share of live cases with |break(log J/O*, 1) − t_b| ≤ 1; the null: the same share with each case's t_b replaced by another live case's t_b of this set (2,000 shuffles), its 95th percentile. Interval above the 95th percentile → H_token-event's first clause holds; at or below → H_token-event refuted.
- **Gain (confirms).** The two-step gain into t_b over the case's median gain elsewhere under §2's sign rule, median over cases with the interval: above 1 → the second clause holds; containing or below 1 → H_token-event refuted. H_token-event not refuted only if both clauses hold.
- **E2 (context-event).** J's concentration minus the seed-free mixed-set concentration, paired, cases with at least 6 contexts a side: interval above 0 → H_context-event not refuted; otherwise refuted.
- **Reported, no rule.** E3 (J's and O*'s ratios under the global scale), E4 (the share at the modal checkpoint against its null), the deeper comparison (E1, gain and E2 of SPEC5's cases recomputed by the same code from SPEC5's bank), the per-context locking share, the calendar-matched percentile of the read's construction, the birth distribution of the new set against the third set's low-layer stratum, the aligned J/O* curves, the clusterer's re-ask rate and the harvest's yield per neuron.

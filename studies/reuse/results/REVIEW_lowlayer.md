## 2. Findings

**F1. The harvest reproduces the harvest.** The capture under the harvest's own condition returns the banked exemplar activations of the known poles to every digit at 14 of 16 loads and within 0.004 at the other two, and the new poles' T_15 values equal the grid's top-50 activations exactly; the dating code returns UNITE's births on 399 existing clusters with 0 mismatches, and the read re-derived the 84 new births with 0 differences. The new poles are the same object as the harvest's, dated by the same rule; the readout reads them under SPEC8's condition, which reproduces SPEC8's own captures exactly on the one known pole that has them (36 contexts, layer 3). The dating and the readout are two instruments: on the 87.6 percent of positions whose harvest prefix exceeds 256 tokens they differ by a median relative difference of 1.15 at T_0, 0.075 at T_9 and 0.022 at T_15 (read), close only late in training.

**F2. The yield matched the plan and the births came late, as on the old stratum.** 84 cases on 41 neurons against the predicted 85 on 45; 0.548 at T_12 or T_13 against the old stratum's 0.603. Layer 0 gives 3 cases of the 84, layers 1 to 3 the rest, so "the low layers" here means layers 1 to 3 in the main; a layer-0 reading needs its own harvest.

**F3. E1 is not separated because the births cluster late, and the gain holds.** With 55 percent of births at two checkpoints, shuffling the births among the cases keeps many breaks within one step of a shuffled date; the null's 95th percentile is 0.405 against the observed 0.44. The gain, which compares the case's own rise into the birth with its rises elsewhere, reads 1.37 [1.09, 1.75], above 1 by its whole interval, and above other cases' same-calendar rises at the 66th percentile, so the rise into the birth is not only the late-training rise that every low-layer ratio shows. H_token-event's two clauses therefore split, and the frozen reading is "not separated".

**F4. The strata differ on E1 and the gain, not on the context-level quantities (exploratory).** Both sets have separate documents; the deeper set is SPEC5's 240 cases read by this spec's code. Low-layer minus deeper by a two-set bootstrap over neurons (read): E1 +0.140 [0.016, 0.263], the gain +0.280 [0.010, 0.666], E2 +0.052 [−0.020, 0.130], the locking +0.048 [−0.045, 0.145]; the deeper set's own locking is 0.143 [0.112, 0.174], above 0. The break's within-one-step share peaks at t_b on the low-layer set (0.440 against 0.381 at −1 and 0.333 at +1) and at t_b − 2 on the deeper set (0.354 against 0.300 at t_b); the gain into t_b + 1 is 1.380 [1.265, 1.701] here and 1.250 [1.175, 1.350] deeper (read). **F6. Spec and process defects, recorded for the next author.** (a) E1's within-set null has little room on a late-born set.

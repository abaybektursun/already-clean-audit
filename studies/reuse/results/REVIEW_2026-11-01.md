## 3. Defects and limits, with mechanism and blast radius

**D1. R3's rise is not specific to O*'s drivers.** The 15 setting splits that are also primary ceilings were read at T_15 by S3 and at the jobs' births by the primary run (17 reads, two splits at two births). I_ceil rises from a median 0.711 at birth to 0.788 at T_15, paired difference median **0.142 [0.039, 0.180]** (bootstrap over splits, seed 1; 13 of 17 rise). R3 is 0.098 [0.068, 0.128]. Mechanism: at birth the top-50 drivers of any job are less concentrated, so any two driver sets read on the same contexts overlap less; by T_15 the same job's two halves share more. Blast radius: the frozen label "consolidation" stands; the reading "reuse grows over training" does not, since the same-job control grows as much. Across all 138 birth ceilings the median is 0.653 against 0.788 for the 65 T_15 setting splits (different neurons, so not paired). Fix: none in budget; a next spec reads R3 against the ceiling's own rise. Exploratory, 2026-11-01.

Addendum 2026-11-01 (amendment 2, A1; `REVIEW_2026-11-01_second.md` F4, recomputed in `scripts/review/second_review.py` → `results/primary/offline2.json`): the mechanism sentence above, "at birth the top-50 drivers of any job are less concentrated", is retracted. Own fall is 0.529 at birth and 0.526 at T_15 over the 147 jobs live at both (paired median −0.009 [−0.023, 0.018], 70 of 147 rise); the cross fall rises 0.112 → 0.182 (paired +0.054 [0.027, 0.061]). The blast radius stands: the same-job control's rise still removes the reuse reading from R3. The reason is not concentration of a job's own drivers but growth in what one context set's drivers do for another context set of the same neuron.

**D2. The planted reference is at T_15 only, and the instrument's separation is thinner at birth.** Room with the jobs' own birth ceilings and the T_15 planted 95th percentile (0.459) is 0.653 − 0.459 = **0.195**, under the frozen 0.200 line; the frozen gate was read at T_15 (0.329) and passed as written. At the matched checkpoint the real jobs' I, 0.375 [0.303, 0.446], is above the planted interval by 0.15, under all three aggregations of §4. Blast radius: R1 "refuted" is the frozen birth-checkpoint reading and decides; the T_15 comparison is the like-for-like one and says the opposite. Both are reported in `RESULTS.md`. SPEC §8 and §13.5 named this limit before the run.

**D3. The job-fall rule can be decided by one context.** The frozen fall is the mean over contexts of the per-context relative fall, so a context with a small a_P before ablation carries a large relative change. `L31N3262-:c2` at T_11 has eleven contexts with a_P 12.4–31.8 and own falls 0.04–0.47, and one (`ate` in "Streamate", a_P 0.768) with own −1.43 and cross −4.56; the job's I is −2.39 (`results/primary/1/t11/samples/arms.jsonl`). Blast radius: none on any verdict. Under an activation-weighted fall ((Σa_before − Σa_after)/Σa_before) R1 is 0.232 [0.184, 0.290] against planted 0.172 [0.116, 0.224], R2 0.082, R3 0.106, R4 0.196 [0.110, 0.271], and no job cancels; under the median per-context fall R1 is 0.192 [0.142, 0.273] against 0.175 [0.101, 0.221], R2 0.065, R3 0.095, R4 0.238. Every verdict is unchanged (`scratch review_extras.py`, numbers in the journal). 18 of 148 live jobs (12%) have I < 0; 25 (17%) have I > 0.5.

**D4. R2 is specific but small in absolute terms.** At birth the cross fall's median is 0.113 (quartiles 0.045–0.220) against foreign 0.010 (−0.007–0.042) and random 0.007. The difference shrinks with later births: 0.25 at T_9 (n 18), 0.12 at T_10, 0.10 at T_11, 0.05 at T_12 and T_13. Not a defect; the magnitude belongs beside the verdict.

## 8. What this means for the decision in SPEC §1

The answer to the question is no at birth and specific: R1 refuted, R2 positive. By §1 the input-reuse reading of growth fails on O7 at top-50 for the drawn set, and the next study asks whether what is shared is the output side. The review adds one thing the decision rule did not anticipate: the refutation is carried by the 93 unrelated jobs, which sit in the planted band, while the 57 related jobs reuse the old job's drivers at 0.38, above it, and this split holds in every stratum. Eliwat's "unrelated facets share no drivers at formation" is reproduced on a 7B SwiGLU model; "related facets do" is the new half. A next spec's Phase 0 should carry: the label-conditioned reuse, the generic rise of driver concentration over training (D1), the birth-checkpoint reference that this study lacked (D2), and the output-side question §1 names.

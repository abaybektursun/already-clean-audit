## 2. Findings

**F1. The frozen gates failed on their definitions, and the amendment is a post-unblinding redefinition of the quantity every rule reads.** The scale gate compared a 256-token capture with a full-window bank value; the anchor gate's 1e-2 per case was tighter than cross-card bfloat16 on single cases (17 of 240 over the line, maximum 0.028). The amendment captures the scale with the case captures' window at every load, gates the two runs' T_15 captures against each other (equal to the last bit) and the anchor on the median (0.0026). It changes no rule's wording, but s_P(t) sets r_J, r_O and validity, so every rule's input changed after the rules had been computed under the frozen scales (`recount_fable.txt`, journal 17:40 CST). By rule 5 of this repo the 5b readings are an exploratory deviation and the frozen verdict decides: instrument artifact. The bank comparison the amendment reports (median relative difference 48% at T_1, 16% at T_4, 1.3% at T_15) is itself unexplained: no artifact captures the same contexts under both windows with one code, so "the window" is an explanation to test, not a finding.

**F2. D1's doubling pairs are early-training growth, not births, and the share itself depends on the scale statistic.** Half the cases have a doubling pair under the median scale, but 9 to 10% of the pairs end at t_b, all but three fall before it, and 62 of 74 and 44 of 51 cases with a pair have every pair before t_b−1. 48 of the 117 primary pairs start in T_1 to T_4, where the valid share is 0.54 to 0.81 and the scale is a median 0.0014 to 0.30 of the pole's own peak (read, X2). The restriction to pairs from T_9 on reads 0.19 and 0.20. Under the mean of the same 50 contexts the share is 0.58 and 0.64, above 0.5 by its interval on both sets, so the rule's outcome is a property of the choice of median over mean, which the spec fixed without a control on it (§6's global scale was registered as the control and not delivered).

**F3. Stated without a scale, the pole's activation on the new cluster relative to the old one rises over the whole grid, and the two-step gain into the birth is not above the same cases' gain elsewhere on the confirmation set.** J/O* aligned to the birth: medians 0.43 to 0.57 from −8 to −1 (on 93 to 149 and 54 to 91 cases), 0.75 and 0.71 at t_b, 0.84 and 0.81 at +1, 0.93 and 0.84 at +2; over the grid 0.37 to 0.60 from T_2 to T_10 and 0.97 and 0.93 at T_15. The two-step gain into t_b is 1.27 [1.15, 1.42] and 1.16 [1.04, 1.32], but the same cases' median two-step gain away from the birth is 1.12 and 1.18, and the gain into the birth over the case's own gain elsewhere is 1.17 [1.06, 1.30] on the primary and 0.97 [0.85, 1.06] on the fresh cases (the sanity checker's construction, `sanity/SANITY_science.md` item 2: 1.21 [1.09, 1.31] and 1.02 [0.90, 1.21]). My first revision of this review and Results 34 and 36 called the gain a birth signature; withdrawn. The birth is UNITE's crossing rule (the cluster's median activation reaches the pole's corpus 99.9th percentile at t and t+1), not "entering the top 50" as every document said until the sanity check; it puts J under that line at t_b−1 and O* above it in 243 of 243 cases, so "J below O* before the birth" is the definition, not a finding. This is SPEC4 Result 30's ratio of ratios (0.77 over one step) on the whole grid. Whether the gain is J rising or O* falling is what the pole's own scale was to decide; the median scale puts the change on O* (0.80, 0.79) and the mean scale on J (1.18, 1.10), and the O* two-step ratio at the birth (0.80) equals its ratio elsewhere on the grid (0.82 to 0.85), a grid-wide trend. My first version of this review and of Results 34 to 36 read the median-scale picture ("the new cluster sits at the pole's scale from the first thousand steps; the birth is the old cluster's decline") as the finding; the read showed it is one scale's picture, that the early medians are medians over a noisy minority (at T_1, 54% and 52% valid, interquartile range of r_J [0.24, 5.34] and [−0.06, 5.83], 36% and 23% in [0.5, 2]), and that r_J and r_O are near 1 at T_15 because J and O* are among the contexts that define the scale. Withdrawn.

**F4. Recount difference, recorded.** The recount's D2 drops cases whose r_J(t_b−2) is at or below zero (6 primary, 6 fresh; a guard of its own, not in the rule), so its J ratio is 0.02 to 0.03 higher on 142 and 85 cases; the verdict follows the rule (valid pairs only, 148 and 91). All twelve excluded cases are upward zero crossings, which the rule as written enters as negative ratios, non-rises; counted as rises the primary J ratio is 1.14 [1.05, 1.22]. Every treatment reads the same outcome.

**F5. D3 had almost no room, and b_act as defined is not a dating.** The maximum of r_J over valid checkpoints lies more than one step before t_b for 122 of 149 and 79 of 91 cases, so D3's share could not have exceeded 0.18 and 0.13; the maximum sits at T_0 to T_4 for 88 and 56 cases, b_act is the first valid checkpoint for 65 and 51 cases and step 0 for 7 and 15. The validity threshold (1% of s_P(T_15), about 0.05% of the pole's peak) admits near-zero scales, and single early r_J values reach 69. The "median of eight grid steps" is a number about the validity rule. The T_9-on variant (0.40 and 0.39) is reported, not ruled.

**F6. Spec defects, for the next author.** (a) The scale gate compared two windows (F1). (b) The anchor tolerance was per case across cards (F1). (c) The scale statistic (median of 50) had no registered control that was delivered; the mean of the same contexts flips D1 and D2 (F2, F3). (d) D1 counts a doubling anywhere on the grid; the share at t_b was reported without a rule and carries the answer. (e) b_act's maximum is taken over all valid checkpoints (F5). (f) The validity threshold is set against s_P(T_15), a sixteenth of the pole's peak. (g) J and O* are drawn from the 50 contexts that define the scale, so the T_15 endpoint is fixed by construction. (h) §6's global-scale statistics could not be formed: the layer's median |ā| is banked at T_15 by this run and at T_5 to T_13 and T_15 by SPEC4, not at T_0 to T_4 or T_14. (i) The §7 GPU row reads the dating run's job wall (1.04 h); the amendment added 1.27 h of job wall and 1.28 h from launch to STATUS. (j) The §7 predicted intervals missed on all four landed rows (D1 0.560, D2 J 0.975, largest ratio 2.27, GPU 1.04), inside their refuted-if bands. (k) §1 defines b_act by half the T_15 value and §2 by half the maximum; the verdict implements §2; the §1 wording gives the same D3 (`explore_scale_variants.json`). (l) The spec and the write-ups called UNITE's birth "enters the top 50"; it is the 99.9th-percentile crossing rule. (m) The frozen output's key `min_valid_share_from_T6` names the pre-amendment start of the validity gate (T_9 in the rule). (n) No literature search is recorded for this spec; the next spec's Phase 0 does it. (o) §9.4's five-case curve check was not done before the frozen verdict; it is banked now (`explore_scale_variants.txt`).

Errata of this review's first version (journal 19:20 CST).** It called the amendment's readings the result; said "neither change touches a rule's definition"; stated the window as the cause of the bank difference; said "the new cluster sits at the pole's scale from the first thousand steps" and "the birth is the old cluster's decline" as findings; gave "12 contexts per cluster" (6 to 12); "180 to 97 poles over 5%" (171 at T_1 to 97 at T_8; 180 is T_0); "(few cases)" at t_b−8 (95 and 58 are valid there); "two outside their predicted intervals" (all four landed rows are); "billed" for launch-to-STATUS intervals; and "same card type and code" (two commits). ## 3. Disposition

Pre-registered outcome: instrument artifact, rules not read. Exploratory: D1 and D2 not separated under the median scale and refuted the other way under the mean scale (and a third way under the median of the contexts outside J and O*: D1 0.69 and 0.57); D3's branch taken on a b_act that early scales set; scale-free, the new cluster rises on the old one over the whole grid with no gain at the birth beyond the trend on the confirmation set (F3). The birth as UNITE defines it is an activation threshold crossing, so the question "is a birth an event" was posed against an object that is an event by construction; the next spec has to choose its object first. Successor, one pre-registration (`NEXT.md` item 7): the layer's median |ā| at every checkpoint as the global scale (a 200-position forward per load, the §6 control this run did not bank), J/O* as the scale-free quantity, rules on the gain at t_b against the grid-wide two-step gain and on J's own trajectory under the global scale, the validity threshold set on the pole's peak, and the two scale statistics both reported.

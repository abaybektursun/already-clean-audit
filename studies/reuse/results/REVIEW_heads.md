## 3. Findings

**F1. The two-neuron null has no room at the head level, and the reason is its upper tail.** Over the 447 finite (pair, load) reads, unweighted, the planted head I has median 0.225, upper quartile 0.558 and 95th percentile 1.654 (the gate's weighted 95th percentile is 1.175); 14% of reads exceed 0.8. The two sides' top-10 head sets overlap by a median 0.10 (3% of reads at 0.5 or more), so the tail is not two neurons listing the same heads. Splitting the cross arm's fall by term on the 63 tail reads (I ≥ 0.8), with the planted unit's two coefficients recovered from the banked g, u and a_P to 1e-6: the fall through side 1's own neuron is a median 0.268 of the response, the fall through λ times side 2's neuron 0.024; side 2's share is a median 0.14 and exceeds 0.5 in 14 of 63. So side 2's top heads lower neuron 1's own activation on neuron 1's contexts. A top-10 overlap of 0.10 does not rule out shared dependence: mean-replacing a head's whole output at every position is a large perturbation with indirect paths, and heads just below the top-10 cut are not counted. Two readings stay open and this bank cannot choose between them: the two-neuron sum is not a usable null for heads as ablated here, or head dependence is generic across neurons at this cut. Named but untested here: the planted sides and λ were fixed at T_15; a ratio with a small own fall is noisy. The frozen rule stops either way.

**F2. What the readable controls say, reported and not ruled.** Positive control: the job's own 10 heads cut its activation by 0.411 at birth (random-matched heads 0.013). Cross − foreign 0.139 [0.090, 0.172] and cross − activity-matched 0.228 [0.189, 0.252], both intervals above 0, against absolute birth falls own 0.420, cross 0.256, foreign 0.100, activity-matched 0.030, random 0.013 over live jobs. I_head at birth 0.693 against I_MLP 0.226 on the same jobs (paired difference 0.396 [0.328, 0.446]). The two jobs' top-10 heads overlap by 0.50; the MLP drivers' top-50 overlap is 0.14, but these are different objects (10 of 32 heads per layer against 50 of 11,008 neurons per layer), and the matched baselines are the planted overlaps, 0.10 for heads against 0.01 for MLP drivers; 443 distinct heads appear in the 150 own sets and the most used head in 27 of them. The one descriptive sentence that survives: at h 10 a neuron's two jobs share half their top heads and the new job's activation depends on the old job's heads more than on another neuron's or on activity-matched heads; whether that exceeds what two unrelated neurons show cannot be decided with this null.

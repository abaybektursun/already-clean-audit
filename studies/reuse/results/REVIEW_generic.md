## 2. Findings

**F1. The held scale as defined is not an instrument at upstream layers, and the bank says why in part.** 209 of 1,730 held files hold a NaN at the pole (1,597 contexts; no free file does; g and u NaN in the same contexts, no inf anywhere). Every NaN file's set holds a member of its layer's top 1% by |ā| (median largest within-layer ratio 244 against 21); with such an outlier in layers 0 to 10, 126 of 136 files with six or more held layers above it are NaN and 5 of 280 with five or fewer; one held layer never produced a NaN. So the held forward runs away by compounding across a run of held layers after an outlier. The clean divisor's value at the positions concerned is not banked, so the near-zero-divisor account the journal gave at 22:50 CST is an inference, not shown. The gates (one layer-4 case's 20 contexts and 3,025 identity-arm contexts that seed no perturbation) could not see a failure that needs a pole at layer 10 or above. Where finite, the held null's 95th percentile (1.455) is one pair (PL15_19, held cross 0.638 against free 0.064), which dominates because tercile 1 kept 6 of 20 pairs; without it the room is 0.177, still under the line. Fixes for a successor: a finiteness gate on every held readout, one held layer at a time, and a floor on the divisor only if a reason for it is shown.

**F2. Like for like, holding the scale raises the median of every arm, in pairs with no high-background neuron as much as in those with one, and the held excess tracks the runaway's predictors.** The unexcluded null 0.130 → 0.188 on the 30 finite pairs (layer 5, no B_t member: 0.039 → 0.115); the 1% null 0.040 → 0.068 on 36; the 3% and per-layer nulls up by 0.009 and 0.004 paired; per unit held > free in 73.6% of 12,477 finite contexts. The excess is 0.114 on the finite contexts of partly-NaN units against 0.016 in wholly finite units and grows with the outlier ratio (Spearman 0.35 to 0.45) and the number of held layers (0.47). So "the norm compensates a replacement" is one reading among several; for a multi-layer set the held fall includes every later held layer's un-normalised response and is not the set's direct contribution. The one-layer arm, free of compounding, rises by 0.007 (41 of 56). What survives holding is the members-alone fall and the gap between the unexcluded and 1% nulls (0.092 free, 0.074 held on the 12 pairs finite in all four conditions, per-pair change +0.004). That argues against the high-background term running through the ablated layers' norm scale and does not establish "direct". My first version of this review and of Result 39 said "raises every fall", "the norm compensates", "the term is direct", and set the free null on 56 pairs beside the held one on 30; all four withdrawn.

**F3. The cuts (free scale, finite everywhere; both rules stamped not read).** The 3% cut lowers the null from 0.067 to 0.026 (Δ 0.029 [0.001, 0.053], from tercile 1 alone; would read not separated at 0.02); it also lowers the pairs' own fall (0.415 → 0.390) and the ceiling under it was not read. The per-layer 1% cut lowers the null to 0.015, but the comparison is unmatched: it removes 550, 1,650 and 2,860 candidates from a layer-5, 15 and 26 pair's pool where the global cut removes 0, 13 and 285, takes 360 and 375 members out of the unexcluded own and cross sets against 129 and 135, and lowers own falls to 0.383 against 0.415. N3's rule has no lower branch. The per-layer held null has no non-finite pair; the 3% held null also clears the room arithmetic, though the ceiling was read on 1%-cut sets. Neither cut is established as the better exclusion.

**F4. Weights alone rank 0.12 of the drivers, exactly the line.** W1 0.12 [0.10, 0.14] (6 of 50 at the median, 48% of cases below; 0.14 on the finite cases), above the two-neuron 95th percentile (0.06), far below the halves' 0.38; shuffled 0.0. By the frozen wording H_weights would read not refuted; the line was set with the birth value (0.08) known. The proxy's 0.70 is largely built in (the attribution score with means substituted on the same contexts) and needs the same captures as D′.

**F5. Spec defects, for the next author.** (a) §2's held scale has no finiteness gate and compounds across held layers. (b) §5 gates all rules on the held instrument, so the free-scale rules are stamped not read for a failure that does not touch their inputs. (c) The W1 failing input cannot be exercised when a gate fails (exit 3). (d) §7's norm-share row assumed the held fall is smaller than the free one. (e) §7's GPU row over-estimated a g6e.4xlarge load by three. (f) SPEC2's `planted_ref` raises on a draw with an all-non-finite tercile. (g) N3 has no lower branch. (h) The spec's "20 contexts" gates are blind to a failure that needs a deep pole. (i) The recount's statistic must equal the verdict's for the recount to verify it; the first version's did not. (j) The per-layer cut needs a matched comparison (same count removed, or own falls matched).

## 3. Disposition

Pre-registered outcome: instrument artifact (the held scale), every rule not read. Exploratory and narrow: the high-background term does not run through the ablated layers' norm scale; the held instrument runs away by compounding and needs a one-layer form with a finiteness gate; the 3% and per-layer cuts lower the free null while also lowering own falls, with no matched comparison; weights alone sit at the W1 line. Decision for the program: keep the model's own norm in every ablation; keep the global 1% exclusion until a matched per-layer comparison is read under a rule; withdraw the method note's mechanism paragraph.
